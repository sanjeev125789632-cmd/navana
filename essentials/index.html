Premium Sanitaryware & Hardware Solutions in Nerul

The tactile quality of a home is defined by its micro-details: the heavy feel of a solid brass door lever, the smooth flow of a waterfall basin mixer, and the quiet precision of concealed thermostatic valves. At NAVANA Essentials, we curate high-end sanitaryware, bath fixtures, and architectural hardware for luxury homeowners, architects, and interior designers across Navi Mumbai.

Located at Shop 53, 19 East, Sector 19A, Nerul, our Essentials division displays an extensive collection of PVD-coated finishes (Brushed Gold, Rose Gold, Matte Black, Gunmetal, and Chrome). All our products undergo stringent hydraulic pressure testing and corrosion resistance verification to withstand daily coastal humidity in Navi Mumbai.

"A luxury bath space relies on flawless tactile touchpoints. We select fittings that elevate routine daily rituals into spa-like wellness experiences."

Our Essentials Product & Supply Portfolio

We source directly from leading international and luxury domestic brassware manufacturers:

  • Designer Faucets & Mixer Suites: Wall-mounted concealed basin mixers, freestanding bathtub fillers, and single-lever waterfall taps.
  • Thermostatic Shower Architecture: Overhead ceiling-recessed rain showers, mist jets, body massage sprays, and digital temperature control panels.
  • Imported Ceramic Sanitaryware: Wall-hung rimless WCs, counter-top vessel basins, ceramic bidets, and matte-finish stone bathtubs.
  • Architectural Door Hardware: Solid brass mortise handles, magnetic latch locks, concealed hinges, and digital smart door locks.
  • Custom Electrical & Lighting Trims: Brushed metal switch plates, dimmable architectural sockets, and trimless LED profiles.

Architectural Specification & On-Site Support

Beyond product supply, our technical team works alongside your plumbing contractors and interior architects, providing technical CAD rough-in drawings and installation guidance to guarantee leak-free performance.
