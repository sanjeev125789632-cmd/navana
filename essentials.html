Common questions

Before contacting the fittings desk

Which brands does NAVANA Essentials supply?

No verified brand list is published on this website. Send the product category and specification to the Essentials desk and ask which brands and models are currently available.

How should faucets and sanitary fittings be selected?

Check water pressure, inlet sizes, basin or counter dimensions, spout reach, concealed-body compatibility and access for maintenance. Confirm that all installation components are included.

Which finishes suit humid rooms?

Performance depends on the base material, coating, cleaning products, ventilation and exposure to water or salts. Ask for the manufacturer’s care instructions and do not assume any finish is maintenance-free.

Can I ask about a finish shown in a reference image?

Yes, but a visual reference may not identify the exact product or finish. Ask for a physical sample, product code and care guidance before ordering.

Does NAVANA provide installation?

Installation is not presented as automatically included. Confirm whether supply, delivery, installation and warranty support apply to the selected product and record them in the written order or proposal.
