NAVANA Essentials FAQs

Which bathroom fittings are suitable for premium homes?

Suitable fittings combine reliable internal components, compatible water pressure, serviceability, warranty support and a finish that works with the room. Review faucet cartridges, aerators, concealed-body access, sanitaryware dimensions and spare-part availability. Premium appearance should not replace practical installation and maintenance checks.

How should bathroom hardware be selected?

Start with the room plan and user needs, then coordinate dimensions, mounting locations, finish, load capacity and corrosion resistance. Confirm the wall substrate and concealed services before drilling. Towel rails, robe hooks, grab bars and accessories should be positioned for comfortable use, not selected only as decorative pieces.

What is architectural hardware?

Architectural hardware includes functional building components such as door handles, hinges, locks, closers, stoppers and related fittings. Selection depends on door material, thickness, weight, frequency of use, security, fire or accessibility requirements and finish. Product specifications should be coordinated with the architect and installer.

Which finishes are suitable for humid environments?

Finish performance depends on the base material, coating system, cleaning products, ventilation and exposure to water or salts. Stainless steel of an appropriate grade and quality plated or coated products are common options, but no finish is maintenance-free. Follow the manufacturer’s care and installation instructions.

How do I choose faucets and sanitary fittings?

Check available water pressure, inlet sizes, basin or counter dimensions, spout reach, flow needs, concealed-body compatibility and after-sales support. Confirm that every component required for installation is included. A coordinated finish helps visually, but technical compatibility and accessible maintenance should guide the decision.

Does NAVANA provide consultation for bathroom fittings?

NAVANA Essentials supports product selection from the NAVANA studio in Nerul. Bring plans, measurements, reference images and the plumbing specification so options can be compared against the design and site. Confirm current availability, lead time, warranty and installation responsibility before placing an order.

Can fittings be coordinated with an interior design project?

Yes. Early coordination helps align sanitaryware sizes, tile setting-out, plumbing points, metal finishes and installation sequencing. NAVANA Essentials can work alongside NAVANA Designs when both form part of the agreed scope, reducing late changes between selection and site execution.
