Common questions

Before contacting the real estate desk

Can I enquire about residential and commercial property?

Yes. NAVANA identifies Haven as its real estate division for residential and commercial enquiries. Share the type of property and intended use so the desk can confirm whether it can assist with your specific requirement.

What should I carry for a property viewing?

Keep your requirement list, photo identification if requested by the property representative, and questions about area, condition, charges, approvals and possession. Do not make a payment or sign documents without completing appropriate independent checks.

How should I shortlist a property?

Start with an all-in budget, required usable area, commute, parking, possession status and non-negotiable needs. Compare the actual layout, light, ventilation, maintenance costs and building condition rather than relying only on brochure descriptions.

What documents should be checked before buying?

Checks can include ownership and title records, approvals, encumbrances, agreement terms, maintenance liabilities, possession status and MahaRERA details where applicable. CIDCO or leasehold properties may require additional checks. Use an independent property lawyer for your transaction.

Does NAVANA provide legal or valuation advice?

The published scope does not establish NAVANA as a legal or valuation professional. Confirm any coordination support in writing, and use appropriately qualified independent advisers for title, legal, tax, technical and valuation checks.
