Refining Interior Architecture Across Nerul & Navi Mumbai

At NAVANA Designs, interior design is not merely decoration—it is the art of spatial architecture. We believe a well-designed room should move seamlessly with the habits of its occupants, offering visual stillness, tactile warmth, and intuitive functionality. Based in Nerul Sector 19A, our interior design studio serves homeowners across Navi Mumbai, crafting bespoke environments for luxury apartments, penthouses, and independent villas.

Our design philosophy balances clean modern aesthetics with rich organic elements: natural veneer woods, muted Italian stone, brushed brass accents, and custom soft furnishings. Every project begins with a deep study of natural daylighting, acoustic flow, and circulation paths to ensure that beauty never compromises living comfort.

"Interior architecture is about bringing harmony between structural enclosure and human living. We replace visual clutter with intentional craftsmanship and organic luxury."

NAVANA Designs FAQs

How much does interior design cost in Navi Mumbai?

Interior cost depends on carpet area, scope, layout changes, materials, hardware, custom furniture and site conditions. A meaningful estimate requires a brief and measurements; a fixed generic price can be misleading. NAVANA Designs can review your requirements and prepare a scope-led proposal before work begins.

How long does a 3BHK interior project take?

The programme depends on design approvals, site readiness, civil work, material lead times and the amount of custom fabrication. A timeline should be prepared only after the scope and site have been reviewed. Keep time for design decisions, procurement, installation, snagging and final handover.

What is turnkey interior design?

Turnkey interior design combines planning and coordinated execution under one defined scope. It may include layouts, material selection, drawings, procurement coordination, custom furniture, services and site work. Confirm the exact inclusions, exclusions, approval stages, payment milestones and warranty responsibilities in the project proposal.

Does NAVANA handle design and execution?

NAVANA Designs presents itself as an interior planning and execution division within NAVANA. The exact services depend on the property and agreed project scope. During consultation, confirm whether civil work, electrical, plumbing, loose furniture, appliances, styling and approvals are included in your written proposal.

How do I choose materials for a premium interior?

Choose materials for performance first: moisture exposure, cleaning, sunlight, load, indoor air quality and expected maintenance. Then compare appearance, samples, warranties and lifecycle cost. A coordinated palette should work across flooring, cabinetry, walls, metals and fabrics without sacrificing suitability for the room.

What is included in an interior design consultation?

A useful initial consultation typically covers your brief, rooms, functional needs, visual preferences, budget range, timeline and site constraints. Bring a floor plan and reference images if available. NAVANA Designs can then identify the next measurement, concept and proposal steps relevant to your project.

Can kitchens, wardrobes and bathroom finishes be coordinated together?

Coordinating these elements early helps align colours, hardware, storage, services and procurement. NAVANA Designs can connect the interior brief with NAVANA Kitchens and NAVANA Essentials, subject to the agreed scope, so decisions are reviewed as one system.
