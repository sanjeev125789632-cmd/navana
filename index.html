Starting an enquiry

A practical first conversation

You do not need a finished brief. Share what you know, and the relevant desk can identify what needs to be confirmed next.

How NAVANA is organised

  1. Choose the relevant division

    Property, interiors, kitchens and fittings have separate contact numbers and requirement details.

  2. Share the available information

    Include your location, type of space, measurements or plans, intended scope and any reference material you already have.

  3. Confirm scope before proceeding

    Ask for written confirmation of services, responsibilities, availability, costs and next steps. Installation or execution should be treated as included only when confirmed in the proposal.
