Build the brief

Start with people, rooms and constraints

A useful design discussion is more specific than a preferred style. It should identify who uses the space, what must be stored, what can change and what must remain.

Common questions

Before an interior consultation

What should I bring to the first discussion?

Bring the latest floor plan, site photographs, room list, reference images, budget range and target dates. If the property is occupied, note what will remain and any work-hour restrictions.

How is an interior estimate prepared?

Cost depends on the measured area, rooms included, site condition, materials, hardware, services and custom work. A useful estimate needs a defined brief and stated assumptions; ask for inclusions, exclusions and change-control terms in writing.

How should interior materials be compared?

Review samples for moisture, cleaning, sunlight, load, indoor use and maintenance before comparing colour and texture. Ask for the product reference, warranty and care information for every approved material.

Can NAVANA Designs handle execution?

The exact service must be confirmed for your project. Treat execution, procurement, site supervision and installation as included only when they are listed in the written proposal with clear responsibilities.

Can kitchen and bathroom decisions be coordinated?

The divisions can be discussed together when relevant. Use NAVANA Kitchens for kitchen-specific planning and NAVANA Essentials for fittings, then confirm how coordination will be handled in the agreed scope.
