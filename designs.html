02. DESIGNS DIVISION

Bespoke Interior Design & Architectural Space Planning in Navi Mumbai

"Where properties breathe and designs live!" Transforming residential apartments, villas, and executive suites into warm, timeless sanctuaries tailored to human rhythm.

Refining Interior Architecture Across Nerul & Navi Mumbai

At NAVANA Designs, interior design is not merely decoration—it is the art of spatial architecture. We believe a well-designed room should move seamlessly with the habits of its occupants, offering visual stillness, tactile warmth, and intuitive functionality. Based in Nerul Sector 19A, our interior design studio serves homeowners across Navi Mumbai, crafting bespoke environments for luxury apartments, penthouses, and independent villas.

Our design philosophy balances clean modern aesthetics with rich organic elements: natural veneer woods, muted Italian stone, brushed brass accents, and custom soft furnishings. Every project begins with a deep study of natural daylighting, acoustic flow, and circulation paths to ensure that beauty never compromises living comfort.

"Interior architecture is about bringing harmony between structural enclosure and human living. We replace visual clutter with intentional craftsmanship and organic luxury."

Our Interior Design Services

We manage full turnkey interior execution, taking your vision from 3D conceptualization to flawless site handover.

  • Turnkey Residential Interiors: Complete interior transformation for 2BHK, 3BHK, 4BHK, and luxury duplex residences in Navi Mumbai.
  • Architectural Space Planning: Layout optimization, wall re-alignments, micro-zoning, and structural circulation design.
  • Bespoke Carpentry & Casework: Custom wardrobes, TV console units, fluted wood paneling, and handcrafted timber furniture.
  • Architectural Lighting & Automation: Layered ambient lighting, cove accentuation, smart scene controllers, and mood dimming systems.
  • Material & Soft Curation: Selection of imported marble, Italian tiles, veneer finishes, custom drapes, and textured wall coverings.

The NAVANA Design Process

Every interior project moves through structured phases: Discovery & Concept Layouts, Photorealistic 3D Visuals, Material Selection at our Nerul Studio, and Precision Site Execution under strictly supervised engineering standards.
