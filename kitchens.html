Layout discussion

Common starting layouts

The suitable layout depends on the room dimensions, openings, services, workflow and clear movement space.

Straight

One working run for a compact room or open wall.

Parallel

Two facing runs where the clear aisle permits comfortable use.

L-shaped

Two connected walls, often leaving one side more open.

U-shaped

Three working sides where room width and access allow.

Common questions

Before a kitchen consultation

Do I need final measurements before enquiring?

No. Approximate dimensions or a floor plan are enough for an initial discussion. Final site-verified dimensions are required before components are produced or ordered.

What affects a modular kitchen estimate?

Cost depends on measured cabinet area, internal material, shutter finish, hardware, storage accessories, worktop, appliances and site conditions. Compare quotations against the same written specification rather than the total price alone.

How should I compare worktop materials?

Review heat, stain, scratch, joint, edge and maintenance requirements. Natural and engineered surfaces behave differently; confirm the exact material, thickness, support, care instructions and warranty before approval.

How long does the process take?

Timing depends on measurement approval, design sign-off, material availability, production, site readiness and worktop or appliance coordination. Ask for a schedule separating design, production, delivery, installation and snag resolution.

Can existing appliances be included in the plan?

Yes, provide each appliance’s exact make, model and installation dimensions. The proposal should confirm required clearances, service points and who is responsible for appliance installation.

Does NAVANA install modular kitchens?

Installation is not presented as automatically included. Ask the Kitchens desk to state supply, delivery, installation, countertop work, appliance fitting and service responsibilities in the written proposal.
