NAVANA Kitchens FAQs

How much does a modular kitchen cost in Navi Mumbai?

Cost varies with layout length, cabinet carcass and shutter materials, hardware, internal accessories, countertop, appliances and site work. Accurate pricing needs measurements and a defined specification. Ask for an itemised proposal that makes material grades, hardware ranges, inclusions and exclusions easy to compare.

What is the difference between plywood and MDF kitchens?

Plywood is made from bonded wood veneers and is generally chosen where moisture resistance and screw holding are priorities. MDF has a smooth, uniform surface that suits painted or routed finishes but requires appropriate moisture protection. The best choice can differ for carcasses, shutters and dry-area panels.

Is BWP plywood suitable for modular kitchens?

BWP-grade plywood is commonly considered for moisture-prone kitchen cabinetry when the product genuinely meets the stated standard and edges are properly sealed. Material grade alone does not ensure durability: fabrication, edge treatment, sink-area detailing, plumbing and ventilation also matter. Verify manufacturer documentation before selection.

Blum vs Hettich: which kitchen hardware is better?

Both brands offer multiple product ranges, so compare like-for-like hinges, drawer systems, lift-ups, load ratings, warranties and local service rather than only the logo. The better option is the system that fits the cabinet design, usage level and budget. Confirm exact model numbers in the quotation.

Quartz vs granite: which is better for kitchen countertops?

Engineered quartz offers consistent colour and low routine sealing needs, while natural granite is heat resistant and visually unique but may require periodic sealing depending on the stone. Both need correct support and installation. Review heat, stain, edge, joint and maintenance requirements before choosing.

How long does modular kitchen installation take?

Timing depends on measurement approval, design sign-off, material availability, factory production, site readiness and countertop or appliance coordination. On-site installation is only one part of the programme. Request a written schedule that separates design, production, delivery, installation and snag resolution.

Which kitchen layouts work well for Navi Mumbai apartments?

Straight and parallel layouts can suit compact spaces, L-shaped layouts retain an open corner, and U-shaped layouts maximise work surfaces where width permits. Islands need comfortable circulation and service planning. NAVANA Kitchens can assess the room measurements, doors, windows and plumbing before recommending a layout.
