About NAVANA

One business, four clearly defined points of contact.

NAVANA is based in Nerul, Navi Mumbai. Its four divisions cover real estate enquiries, interior design, modular kitchens and architectural fittings.

Why the divisions are separate

A property search begins with location, budget and space requirements. An interior project begins with a brief and site information. A kitchen needs measurements and appliance details. Fittings need specifications and availability checks. NAVANA keeps these conversations separate so customers can reach the right desk without navigating unrelated services.

What customers should confirm

Services can vary by requirement. Before proceeding, ask for a written proposal that identifies the agreed scope, inclusions, exclusions, responsibilities, availability, price and next steps. Installation, site execution, legal review or supply should not be assumed unless it appears in the written scope.
