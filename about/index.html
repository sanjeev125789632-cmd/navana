ABOUT NAVANA

One NAVANA, Four Connected Divisions

NAVANA is a multidisciplinary property and design brand based in Nerul, Navi Mumbai. Its divisions cover property consultancy, interior design, modular kitchens, bathroom fittings and architectural hardware.

What NAVANA Does

NAVANA connects decisions that often happen separately: finding a property, planning its interiors, designing the kitchen and selecting bathroom or architectural fittings. Clients can approach one division independently or explore connected support across the group.

How the Process Starts

Start by sharing your property or project requirement, preferred location, available plans, target scope and timeline. The relevant NAVANA desk can then clarify the next consultation, measurement, selection or site-review step. Written proposals should define the exact scope, inclusions, exclusions and responsibilities.
